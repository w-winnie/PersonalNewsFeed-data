### Major Themes in Recent Astrophysics News

1. Exoplanet Discoveries and Habitability

The exploration of exoplanets continues to reveal intriguing candidates for habitability, with the recent discovery of TOI-1422 c, a planet nearly three times the size of Earth located 500 light-years away. This finding enhances our understanding of diverse planetary systems and raises questions about the potential for life in environments previously deemed inhospitable. The ongoing investigation into technosignatures—indicators of advanced extraterrestrial civilizations—further emphasizes the urgency and significance of these discoveries, as scientists also turn their attention to double star systems that may support life despite the absence of large exoplanets.
