#### Galactic Insights: Baby Planet and Ghost Galaxies
Recent findings have unveiled Elias 2-24 b, the youngest known planet, at under a million years old, providing new insights into planetary formation processes. Additionally, the discovery of nearly 50 "ghost galaxies" challenges existing models of galaxy formation and distribution. These faint galaxies, difficult to detect due to their low luminosity, suggest a need for a reevaluation of our understanding of dwarf galaxies, which are believed to be numerous in the universe. This research highlights the complexities of galaxy evolution and the need for refined observational techniques.
