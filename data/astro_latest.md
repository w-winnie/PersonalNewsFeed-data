#### Stellar Fate and Planetary Consumption
Recent studies have illuminated the dramatic consequences of stellar evolution on nearby planetary systems, particularly as stars transition into red giants. Observations suggest that these expanding stars can engulf close-orbiting giant planets, leading to their destruction. This phenomenon underscores the turbulent dynamics of planetary systems as they reach their end stages. Notably, the scarcity of close-in giant planets around evolved stars implies that many may have been consumed, offering new insights into the life cycles of planetary systems and the ultimate fate of celestial bodies.
