Recent research has revealed critical insights into how solar activity influences satellite reentries, particularly during periods of increased sunspot activity. As satellite constellations like SpaceX's Starlink proliferate in low Earth orbit, understanding these solar cycles becomes essential for predicting orbital decay and ensuring operational safety. The findings highlight the necessity for improved solar monitoring to mitigate risks associated with satellite operations.
