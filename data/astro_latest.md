The James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) has made a groundbreaking discovery by detecting the most distant supernova ever recorded, which occurred less than a billion years after the Big Bang. This observation, confirmed through a gamma-ray burst, not only provides a unique window into early stellar evolution but also suggests that the fundamental mechanisms of supernovae have remained consistent throughout the universe's history.
